# 🇺🇸 English: Legal Disclaimers

### 1. Strictly Administrative & Non-Clinical Scope (Non-HIPAA)
Abrevia is designed solely as an administrative, customer care, and operational communication technology. It is **not** engineered or intended for use by covered healthcare entities to store, transmit, or process Protected Health Information (PHI) under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) or ACA Section 1557. Organizations requiring HIPAA-compliant clinical messaging must not use this version of Abrevia for medical diagnosis or clinical patient care records.

### 2. Automated AI Translation & Human-In-The-Loop Advisory
Abrevia utilizes state-of-the-art neural language models (Google Gemini) for real-time translation between English and Spanish. While translation quality is high, automated translation systems can produce errors, idioms, or colloquial discrepancies. Users transmitting legally significant notifications, court summons, or official disciplinary records are advised to use the **Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) pre-send review** feature or retain a certified human interpreter.

### 3. Federal Title VI & Executive Order 13166 Language Access Guidance
Abrevia provides zero-download SMS communication tools and immutable audit records that assist federally funded programs (Education, Judicial, Public Safety, Transit, HUD, Social Services, FEMA) in meeting their Title VI language access obligations to Limited English Proficient (LEP) constituents. The deployment of Abrevia does not automatically constitute full statutory compliance; each agency is responsible for establishing comprehensive Language Access Plans (LAPs) as required by federal guidelines.

### 4. Carrier Deliverability & Third-Party Telecommunications
SMS delivery relies on underlying commercial cellular networks and gateway hardware. Message delivery times and receipt may be affected by carrier network congestion, recipient device availability, cellular signal coverage, or local telecommunications regulations.

### 5. Complete Exoneration of Developers, Agents, Employees & Representatives
Under no circumstances shall the software developers, computer programmers, system architects, founders, employees, directors, officers, agents, representatives, contractors, or affiliates of Abrevia or Servicios ADG be held liable in any way, under any jurisdiction or cause of action, for any damages, legal liabilities, fines, penalties, operational interruptions, lost revenues, translation inaccuracies, missed judicial deadlines, or carrier-level transmission failures. The technology is provided strictly on an "as-is" administrative basis. Each organization, deploying agency, and individual user assumes sole and exclusive legal and operational responsibility for communications sent, received, or relied upon through this platform.

# 🇲🇽 Español: Deslindes Legales

### 1. Ámbito Estrictamente Administrativo y No Clínico (No Sujeto a HIPAA)
Abrevia ha sido concebida exclusivamente para operaciones administrativas, atención a clientes y logística de campo. **No** está diseñada ni autorizada para que entidades de salud procesen, transmitan o almacenen Información de Salud Protegida (PHI) sujeta a las regulaciones de la Ley HIPAA de EE. UU. o la Sección 1557 de ACA.

### 2. Aviso sobre Traducción por Inteligencia Artificial y Supervisión Humana
Abrevia emplea modelos neuronales avanzados (Google Gemini) para la traducción instantánea entre inglés y español. Aunque el nivel de fidelidad técnica es sobresaliente, los sistemas automáticos pueden presentar discrepancias idiomáticas. Para notificaciones judiciales o avisos formales vinculantes, se recomienda emplear la opción de **revisión previa con intervención humana (HITL)**.

### 3. Título VI Federal y Orden Ejecutiva 13166
Abrevia proporciona herramientas técnicas sin descargas y bitácoras inmutables para facilitar que entidades con subvenciones federales (Educación, Tribunales, Seguridad Pública, Tránsito, Vivienda HUD, Asistencia Social, FEMA) brinden acceso lingüístico a personas con Dominio Limitado del Inglés (LEP). El uso de Abrevia es un instrumento de apoyo y no exime a cada organismo de formular su Plan de Acceso Lingüístico (LAP) conforme a derecho.

### 4. Redes Celulares y Entrega de Mensajes
La entrega de mensajes de texto SMS depende de las redes de telefonía celular y pasarelas de comunicación. La velocidad y confirmación de recepción pueden verse condicionadas por la cobertura de señal, congestión de antenas o políticas de los operadores telefónicos.
