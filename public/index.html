🏛️ Title VI (Title 6) & LEP Language Access Compliance

Under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. § 2000d) and Executive Order 13166, all recipients of federal funding (transportation, logistics, housing authorities, municipal contractors, and public agencies) are legally mandated to provide meaningful language access to Limited English Proficient (LEP) individuals.

  • Zero-Download Access: LEP recipients text naturally or use frictionless mobile links with no app downloads required.
  • Auditable Compliance Trails: Immutable timestamped bilingual logs demonstrate active compliance under federal review.
  • Human Oversight: Pre-send proofreading (HITL) and certified live LSP interpreters for critical operational notices.

Abrevia is engineered for HIPAA Privacy and Security Rule compliance (45 CFR Parts 160 & 164). Covered Entities can download a pre-signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with AES-256 KMS hardware encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and Title VI compliance logging.

Active HIPAA Security Safeguards (45 CFR § 164.312):
  • Pre-Signed BAA On-Demand: Standard Business Associate Agreements under 45 CFR § 164.502(e) & § 164.504(e).
  • AES-256 KMS Hardware Encryption: Persistent databases and attachments encrypted at rest on AWS block storage.
  • Enforced TLS 1.3 Transmission Security: Modern AES-GCM ciphers protecting all transit between operator, edge & carrier.
  • On-Demand Certified Live Interpreters: Accredited LSP network access for clinical and urgent communication.
