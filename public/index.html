🏛️ Title VI (Title 6) & LEP Language Access Compliance

Under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. § 2000d) and Executive Order 13166, all recipients of federal funding (transportation, logistics, housing authorities, municipal contractors, and public agencies) are legally mandated to provide meaningful language access to Limited English Proficient (LEP) individuals.

  • Zero-Download Access: LEP recipients text naturally or use frictionless mobile links with no app downloads required.
  • Auditable Compliance Trails: Immutable timestamped bilingual logs demonstrate active compliance under federal review.
  • Human Oversight: Pre-send proofreading (HITL) and certified live LSP interpreters for critical operational notices.

Abrevia is strictly an administrative operations tool built for non-medical communication across logistics, property management, retail, and field services.
